    Fiscal & Budget

    Debt held by the public rose from 47% of GDP at the end of FY1993 (OMB via FRED) to 99.0% at the end of FY2025 and 100% at the end of FY2026 (CBO, Nov 9, 2026) and is projected by CBO to reach 120% by 2036; it fell under only one of the six administrations (Clinton), and the two largest single-year jumps (FY2009, FY2020) followed shocks that no administration chose.
