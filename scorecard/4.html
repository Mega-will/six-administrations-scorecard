    Foreign Policy & Alliances

    The clearest pattern across six administrations is a swing from institution-building to unilateral leverage: NATO grew from 16 to 32 members (1999–2024) and allies meeting the 2%-of-GDP guideline rose from 3 (2014) to all 31 with armed forces (2025 est.), while U.S. membership in multilateral bodies flipped with each party change, ending in a January 2026 withdrawal from 66 organizations.
