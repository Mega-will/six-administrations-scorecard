    Six Administrations Scorecard · Bucket 2 of 10

    Economy, Jobs & Monetary

    Real median household income reached a record $87,460 in 2025 after rising under every administration except Bush 43 (−1.8%), yet the top 1% share of household wealth climbed from 24.0% (1993) to 32.5% (Q2 2026) through all six — so each administration's record is defined less by its own laws than by the shock it inherited or met (2001, 2008, 2020, 2021–22 inflation, 2026 energy) and by what the Federal Reserve did about it.
