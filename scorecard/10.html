    Elections, Civic Trust & Federalism

    Trust in the federal government to do what is right "most of the time" or "just about always" was 44% in October 2000, 55–60% just after 9/11, and 17% in September 2025, and across all six administrations the largest changes to election rules, civil-service protections and federal–state power were made by the Supreme Court or by party-line majorities rather than by laws both parties voted for (Pew).
