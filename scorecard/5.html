    Six Administrations Scorecard · Bucket 5 of 10

    Borders, Immigration & Citizenship

    Southwest-border apprehensions have swung by more than an order of magnitude across these six presidencies — 1.64 million in FY2000, 2.21 million in FY2022, 98,708 in FY2026 — while the legal system underneath (a 140,000-a-year employment green-card cap set in 1990, a 65,000 H-1B cap set in 1990, an immigration-court backlog that grew from roughly 186,000 cases in 2009 to a peak of 3,747,675 at the end of Dec 2024 (TRAC) and stood at about 3.0 million in Sept 2026) has been changed almost entirely by executive action and court rulings rather than by statute, because every comprehensive bill since 1996 (2006, 2007, 2013, 2024) died in one chamber.

    5.1

    Measures whether the state controls who enters between ports of entry: Border Patrol apprehensions, barrier miles and agent strength — the most basic test of territorial sovereignty.

    Measures whether the state can stop the criminal flows — people-smuggling, trafficking and synthetic drugs — that exploit the same border, the lethal end of immigration policy.
