    Six Administrations Scorecard · Bucket 9 of 10

    Public Health, Safety & Social Insurance

    The dominant pattern in this bucket is lag: the all-ages uninsured rate fell from 16.0% (2010) to a record 7.6% (2023) and has since turned up to 8.3% (2025), drug-overdose deaths climbed from 25,785 (2003) to 107,941 (2022) and then fell to 70,646 reported deaths for 2025 (provisional, CDC data as of Oct 4, 2026; the final 2024 count was 79,384), and the Social Security retirement trust fund's projected depletion date moved from 2033 to late 2032 in a single year — in every case the laws and shocks that moved the numbers belonged to one administration and the measured outcome showed up under the next.
